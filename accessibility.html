Accessibility Service Prominent Disclosure

Stellar Launcher for Android (app.stellar.launcher) · Unedited screen recording

The accessibility service is optional and off by default. It does one thing: lock the screen when you double-tap the empty home screen, with fingerprint unlock still working. It reads, collects and shares no personal or sensitive data.

What the recording shows

  • App launch & navigation. Opening Stellar Launcher, then Settings → Behaviour → “Double tap locks screen”.
  • In-app Prominent Disclosure (LockAccessDialog). Shown before any system settings open. It explains why an AccessibilityService is required — to call GLOBAL_ACTION_LOCK_SCREEN while keeping fingerprint unlock working — and how to enable "Stellar: lock on double tap".
  • Decline / Cancel. Tapping Cancel leaves the service disabled and returns to Stellar Settings; the row stays Off.
  • Consent / Enable. Tapping “Double tap locks screen” → Open settings → turning on "Stellar: lock on double tap" in Android Accessibility settings → returning to Stellar (On) → double-tapping the empty home screen locks the screen.
  • Data & privacy guarantees. The service is declared with accessibilityEventTypes="", canRetrieveWindowContent="false" and isAccessibilityTool="false". It receives no accessibility events, cannot read any window content, and reads, collects or shares zero personal or sensitive user data.

You can turn the service off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings. Full details: Privacy Policy.
